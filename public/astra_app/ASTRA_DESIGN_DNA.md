# ASTRA DESIGN & ILLUSTRATION DNA

> **SOVIET CINEMATIC COSMIC NOSTALGIA**  
> Канонический документ арт-дирекшна и визуальной экосистемы проекта **АСТРА (Astra Cosmic Wellness)**.

---

### Главное различие блоков
- **Design DNA** отвечает за композицию, фон, палитру, физику света, текстуры и единый визуальный язык всей экосистемы.
- **Illustration DNA** отвечает за лица, персонажей, характер света внутри медальонов, индивидуальную цветовую атмосферу и качество самих изображений.

---

## 1. DESIGN DNA — ОБЩАЯ ДНК ДИЗАЙНА

### DESIGN DNA — SOVIET CINEMATIC COSMIC NOSTALGIA

**Core aesthetic:** Cinematic 1970s Soviet fantasy film, analog film photography, nostalgic celestial romanticism, vintage editorial art direction.

#### Composition
- Wide 16:9 horizontal canvas.
- Solid near-black navy background `#161822`.
- Generous negative space and precise alignment.
- Circular portrait medallions arranged in a single horizontal row.
- Equal circle dimensions, consistent spacing, clean silhouettes.
- Restrained, premium editorial presentation.
- No overlapping portraits, no merged circles, no decorative clutter.

#### Color DNA
- **Midnight charcoal:** `#161822`
- **Warm champagne:** `#F7D794`
- **Coral peach:** `#F59E8E`
- **Seafoam mint:** `#8EC7B8`
- **Cosmic lavender:** `#B89BD6`
- **Burnished amber:** `#AB6A4F`

#### Lighting DNA
- Warm analog halation.
- Soft Pro-Mist diffusion.
- Luminous pastel light wrapping around subjects.
- Gentle cinematic lens bloom.
- Golden-hour highlights balanced with cool celestial undertones.
- Smooth tonal transitions and softly lifted shadows.

#### Material & Texture
- Authentic 1970s film grain.
- Kodachrome / Sovcolor-inspired color response.
- Subtle analog imperfections.
- Soft photographic diffusion.
- Natural skin texture.
- Delicate optical glow rather than digital effects.

#### Art Direction
- Soviet cinema meets poetic cosmic fantasy.
- Intimate, soulful, timeless, mysterious and romantic.
- Authentic vintage photographic character, not contemporary commercial photography.
- Refined visual consistency across all five portraits.

#### Strict exclusions
Modern stock-photo aesthetics, HDR, razor-sharp digital contrast, plastic skin, 3D rendering, clay-like surfaces, excessive retouching, hard artificial shadows, contemporary fashion, random typography, inconsistent framing and merged circular portraits.

## 2. ILLUSTRATION DNA — ДНК ИМЕННО ИЛЛЮСТРАЦИЙ

### ILLUSTRATION DNA — FIVE CELESTIAL CINEMA PORTRAITS

**Image type:** Five distinct cinematic portrait illustrations with a photorealistic vintage-film appearance, each contained within a perfectly circular medallion.

#### Reference fidelity
Match the provided reference's visual language: large circular face portraits, dark uninterrupted background, soft edges, warm faded colors, subtle film grain, dreamy atmospheric overlays and restrained 1970s Soviet cinema nostalgia.

#### Portrait construction
- One clearly recognizable individual per circle.
- Head-and-shoulders or close-up portrait.
- Faces occupy approximately 65–75% of each circle.
- Natural facial anatomy, expressive eyes and believable hair.
- Softly diffused skin, authentic smiles and candid cinematic expressions.
- Consistent camera distance, portrait scale and visual finish.
- Each portrait has its own atmosphere while belonging to the same visual universe.

#### Shared illustration rules
All five portraits must look as though they were photographed on the same vintage cinema camera and developed on the same analog film stock. Preserve individual faces, believable proportions, soft optical bloom, subtle grain, natural expressions and distinct character-specific color atmospheres.

#### Negative prompt
Modern stock photography, commercial beauty advertising, artificial toothy smiles, plastic skin, excessive makeup, CGI, 3D render, cartoon, clay, anime, vector illustration, over-sharpening, HDR, hard digital edges, gloomy horror lighting, exaggerated facial features, duplicated faces, merged circles, cropped heads, inconsistent portrait scale, text inside portraits, excessive particles obscuring faces.
